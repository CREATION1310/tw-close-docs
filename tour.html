  1. Underwriting Decision — every decision, approvals and declines, with grade and conditions. Filed the day it's made.
  2. Funding Confirmation Call — mandatory before any wire: terms and specified percentage confirmed verbally, recording reference logged (never the audio file).
  3. Broker Touch — every referral-partner conversation, topic and summary.

Plain notes are for everything else: facts, dates, amounts, quotes. Write each one as if the merchant will read it — they can lawfully ask to.
