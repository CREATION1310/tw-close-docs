§1Purpose & principles

Trade Wings Capital purchases future receivables from Canadian merchants. We are not a bank and we do not lend — and our records must never suggest otherwise. Three principles govern everything in this document:

  1. Close is the engagement layer. Prospecting, applications, underwriting communication, offers, and renewals live in Close. Money movement, remittance, and servicing live in Kesef (use the Open in Kesef button on any lead).
  2. Every record is a legal record. Notes, calls, emails, and field entries are discoverable. Write facts, in our vocabulary, as if opposing counsel will read them — clean records are what protect the company and the merchant.
  3. Merchants are two tracks. Cooperative merchants get service and renewal offers; a merchant showing remittance friction moves to Watch — Remittance Risk and gets escalated, not chased harder. Selling and pressure never mix.

§3Language rules

We purchase receivables at a discount; the merchant remits a specified percentage of future sales until the purchased amount is retrieved. That structure is why our agreements work under Canadian law — and it collapses if our own records describe it as lending.

purchase price · purchased amountcost of borrowing

Hard rule

The right-hand column never appears in anything you type — notes, emails, SMS, field values, nicknames, anywhere. If a merchant says "so what's the interest on this loan?", answer in our words: "It's a purchase, not a loan — you're selling us $52,000 of future sales for $40,000 today, and there's no interest rate because there's no borrowing." Never repeat their phrasing back.

A receivables purchase has no fixed term. Give estimates only, always labeled as estimates: "most merchants retrieve in roughly eight to ten months, but it moves with your sales."

§4Daily rhythm

Work the queues in this order every morning. The order is triage: committed work before new work.

  1. Queue — Offers Out (Follow-up) — money on the table. Chase signatures and answer objections first.
  2. Queue — Underwriting — files waiting on documents or decisions. Unblock them: one missing bank month stalls everything.
  3. Queue — New Inquiries (Today) — respond same-day, ideally within the hour.
  4. Prospecting — Priority — net-new outreach, worked top-down. The list is sorted by Priority Score; do not cherry-pick.
Before every outbound call

Check Do Not Call on the lead. If it is Yes, stop — no call, no SMS, no exception, regardless of who asks. See §9.

§6Pipelines & stages

Open an opportunity when there is a concrete deal to pursue. Four pipelines, four jobs:

New Funding — a merchant's first advance

Stages run: Application Received → Docs Collection → Underwriting Review → Offer Issued → MPSA Out for Signature → Signed — Pre-Funding Checks → PPSA Registered → Funded.

Hard rule

Nothing reaches Funded before PPSA Registered — the registration number and date must be on the opportunity first. If the wire is urgent, the registration is more urgent.

At Offer Issued, the opportunity must carry Purchase Price, Purchased Amount, Factor Rate, Specified Percentage, and Remittance Frequency. An offer without recorded economics doesn't exist.

Renewals

Eligibility Review → Renewal Offer Issued → MPSA Amendment Out → Funded — Renewal. Check the Renewal record's Retrieval Percent Complete — Derived before offering; renewing too early is a compliance question, not a sales win.

Broker Partnerships

Recruiting referral partners (ISOs). Log every touch as a Broker Touch activity. Partners never get promised approval outcomes.

Funder Placement

Files we place with outside funders when they're not for our book. Each submission is a Funder Submission record — funder name, date, status, offer economics.

§7Logging & activities

If it isn't in Close, it didn't happen. Log every substantive interaction the same day, in the structured activity when one fits:

  • Underwriting Decision — every decision, including declines: decision, internal risk grade, conditions, s.347 screen. The decline you log cleanly today is the discrimination-claim defence five years from now.
  • Funding Confirmation Call — mandatory before every wire: confirm the terms and the specified percentage verbally, record the call, and store the recording reference — never the audio file itself.
  • Broker Touch — every partner conversation: topic and summary.

Free-form notes: facts, dates, amounts, quotes. No speculation about a merchant's character or finances beyond what documents show. Write every note as if the merchant will read it — under Canadian privacy law, they can ask to.

§8Data rules

Never enters Close

Bank statement PDFs · account numbers · SINs · credit bureau reports or scores · card numbers · recordings as files. Close holds derived numbers only — the Bank Statement record takes totals and counts (deposits, NSF count, negative days, balances), nothing else. Documents live in the underwriting document system; Kesef holds the money data.

Field discipline by stage: legal name and Province by Application Out; bank-derived numbers by Underwriting Review; full economics by Offer Issued; PPSA Registration Number and date before Funded. Province drives where we register — get it right the first time.

§9Calls, SMS & email

Three channels, one rule set: every touch comes from your claimed Close identity, lands on the lead's timeline, and reads well in front of a judge. Pick the channel by weight — call for substance, text for logistics, email for detail and documents.

Calls

  • All calls go through your claimed Close number. Personal cells are never used for merchant contact — an off-system call is an off-record call.
  • Calling hours: 9:00–20:00 in the merchant's timezone, Monday–Saturday. No Sunday calls. Close shows the merchant's estimated local time in the dialer — look before you dial.
  • Voicemail counts as a recording: name, company, callback number, under 20 seconds, our vocabulary only.

SMS

  • Text from your claimed number, inside calling hours, for logistics only: confirmations, reschedules, "sent you the checklist," quick nudges. Never discuss terms, amounts, or economics by text.
  • Text only merchants with a live inquiry or existing relationship, identify yourself ("Sharon at Trade Wings"), and answer inbound texts the same business day.
  • Any stop-flavored reply — STOP, unsubscribe, "leave me alone" — is a DNC event: run the procedure below immediately. Close also blocks their SMS automatically.
  • Use approved templates when they exist (Templates ▾ in the composer). Personalize freely; never touch the vocabulary.

Email

  • Email from your Close-connected address for anything with detail: document requests, summaries of a call, next-steps recaps. The thread lives on the lead — no side-channel inboxes.
  • Commercial emails identify Trade Wings and you, and carry an unsubscribe that is honored the moment it's used (that's CASL, and it's not optional).
  • Documents: never invite bank statements into your own thread — point merchants at the underwriting intake channel. If a merchant emails you statements anyway, move them to the document system per §8 and don't re-share them in the thread.
  • Offers and legal terms travel in the MPSA and offer documents — an email restates where to sign, never re-negotiates the numbers.

Do Not Call — the absolute

  • Do Not Call = Yes is absolute across all three channels. These leads are visible in DNC — Never Call so the suppression list is known, not hidden. They are excluded from prospecting; never work around it.
  • If any merchant asks to stop being contacted — in any words, on any channel — set Do Not Call to Yes and fill DNC Reason during the conversation, confirm it back to them ("you won't hear from us again"), and end politely. There is no save attempt on a DNC request.

§11Quick reference

Morning order: Offers Out → Underwriting → New Inquiries → Prospecting — Priority (top-down).

Before any call: DNC check. Before any offer: economics fields filled. Before any wire: PPSA registered + confirmation call logged.

Words: advance, merchant, purchase price, purchased amount, factor rate, specified percentage, remittance, retrieval. Nothing from the never-say column, ever.

When unsure: log the facts, escalate to Jason, and don't delete anything.
